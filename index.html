— Becoming a designer was the best way to keep up with a mind that's constantly looking for how things could work better.

Born in Rio de Janeiro, I grew up on that version of the internet where people shared work freely, forked each other's ideas, and built things together just because they could. That spirit never left me. I still upload high-res photos for anyone to use. I've shared 3D files, left repos open, contributed to things I'd never be credited for. I think that's just how making things should work.

I care about the business side as much as the craft. Maybe more than most designers admit.

Over the years, I've worked on consumer products at national scale: broadcast, banking, telecom. Millions of people, every day. That kind of reach teaches you to think carefully before you ship.

More than scale, what drives me is access.

I want to live in a world where anyone with a phone or a laptop can reach the best products and opportunities. That's what makes the work feel like it matters.
