— Becoming a designer was the best way to keep up with a mind that's constantly looking for how things could work better.

Born in Rio, I grew up on that version of the internet where people shared work freely, forked each other's ideas, and built things together just because they could. That spirit never left me. I still upload high-res photos for anyone to use. I've shared 3D files, left repos open, contributed to things I'd never be credited for. I think that's just how making things should work.

I get curious about most things.

Typography. Photography. Behavioral economics. Why people make the decisions they make (and why they think they made them for different reasons). How businesses scale. How teams break down. How a design system holds up two years after the designer who built it is gone.

I care about the business side as much as the craft. Maybe more than most designers admit.

Over the years, I've worked on consumer products at national scale: broadcast, banking, telecom. Millions of people, every day. That kind of reach teaches you to think carefully before you ship.
